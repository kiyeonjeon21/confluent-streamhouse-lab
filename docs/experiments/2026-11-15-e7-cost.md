This is the cost half of E7.
The operational-complexity log is not written up yet.

## Method

Command, read-only and not billed:

```sh
confluent billing cost list --start-date 2026-11-01 --end-date 2026-11-16 -o json
```

Read at 2026-11-15 14:34 UTC, about 3 hours 20 minutes after the second session's teardown.
The [billing FAQ](https://docs.confluent.io/cloud/current/billing/billing-faq.html) says cost data can take up to 72 hours to reach the Costs API, so re-run the command after 2026-11-18 and update this page if lines were added.

The Costs API returns one line per resource, meter, and day; it has no hourly breakdown.
November had no other lines: everything below belongs to the lab's two environments, created and destroyed on 2026-11-15.

Session times come from the `terraform apply` and `terraform destroy` output of each session (UTC):

| 2 | 10:07 to 11:14 (1 h 7 min) | from about 10:10 to 11:14 | Hybrid read, E2 |

## Observations

- **Flink is the largest line.** Materialized tables run continuously, and each check, event, and snapshot query adds CFU-minutes.
- **Partial hours are billed as full hours.** Tableflow shows it exactly: the topic touched 3 UTC hours in session 1 and 2 in session 2, and was billed 3 and 2 topic-hours.
- **The Basic cluster was billed for eCKUs.** The [pricing page](https://www.confluent.io/confluent-cloud/pricing/) lists Basic as "First eCKU free, then $0.14", yet 8 eCKU-hours were billed at $0.135, with no discount line.
  The clusters touched 3 and 2 UTC hours.
  The counts (5 and 3) match one eCKU for every hour a cluster existed plus one more in each hour a benchmark ran (2 hours in session 1, 1 in session 2).
  That is an inference from daily totals; why the free eCKU did not apply is unknown.
  The [billing dimensions page](https://docs.confluent.io/cloud/current/billing/dimensions/overview.html) says a cluster pays nothing only at zero consumption (no topics, no partitions, no other usage) and otherwise pays at least its minimum capacity.
- **The agent was cheap.** All model inference for the day, all of it in session 1, came to $0.20.
- **The us-east-1 Flink rate is $0.21 per CFU-hour**, lower than the $0.2562 an earlier ap-northeast-2 invoice showed.
